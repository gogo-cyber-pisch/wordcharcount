What is a Character Counter?

A character counter is a free online tool that instantly displays how many characters, words, sentences, and paragraphs your text contains. Our tool analyzes your text in real-time – no signup, no download required.

Why Do You Need a Character Counter?

  • Social Media: Twitter/X (280 characters), Instagram Bio (150), LinkedIn Posts (3,000)
  • SEO: Meta Titles (60 characters), Meta Descriptions (160 characters)
  • Academic: Essays, dissertations, and papers with strict word or character limits
  • Professional: Emails, cover letters, product descriptions, ad copy

How Does This Word Counter Work?

Simply paste or type your text into the box above. Our tool instantly counts characters (with and without spaces), words, sentences, paragraphs, and estimates reading and speaking time. It also shows your top keywords with density percentages and tracks social media character limits in real-time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this character counter completely free?
Yes, 100% free with no registration required. Your text is never stored, saved, or shared with anyone. Everything runs locally in your browser.
Are spaces counted as characters?
We show both counts: characters with spaces and characters without spaces. Most social media platforms count spaces as characters, so keep an eye on the "Characters" count for those limits.
How is reading time calculated?
Reading time is based on an average reading speed of 250 words per minute. Speaking time uses 150 words per minute, which is typical for presentations.
Does this work on mobile devices?
Yes! Our character counter is fully responsive and works perfectly on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.
What is keyword density?
Keyword density shows how often a word appears in your text as a percentage. It's useful for SEO to ensure your content isn't over- or under-optimized for specific keywords. A density of 1-3% is generally recommended.
